💡 Real-Life Step-by-Step Guide: How to Make an ATS-Friendly Resume

Follow these golden rules used by Fortune 500 recruiters and top ATS software (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever):

1. Contact Info Format

Put your Name, Phone, Email, Location (City, State), and LinkedIn link on single lines. DO NOT put contact info inside headers/footers or image graphics — ATS parsers cannot read header text!

2. Google XYZ Bullet Formula

Structure bullet points using Google's formula: "Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". Always quantify results with numbers (%, $, hours saved, team size).

3. Standard Section Headings

Use simple, recognizable section headings: Professional Summary, Professional Experience, Skills, Education. Avoid clever names like "My Journey" or "What I Do".

4. Single-Column Layout

Multi-column tables, sidebars, and graphics scramble text when processed by Taleo/Workday. Always use a clean single-column paper flow.

5. Exact Skill Keywords

Include exact hard skills mentioned in the job post both in your Skills section and inside your Work Experience bullets (e.g. Project Management, SQL, Python).

6. PDF / DOCX Export

Always export your document as a standard vector PDF or clean DOCX file. Avoid image-based PDF scans or PNG image resumes.
